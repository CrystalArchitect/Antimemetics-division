## 1. Core Definitions

### Antimemetics (Proposed, Vision)

**Working definition**: Information-resistant entities, concepts, or phenomena that actively prevent being remembered, communicated, or understood.

Contrasts with **memes** (Dawkins): units of cultural transmission that *spread easily*. Antimemetic entities work *against* transmission.

**Evidence tier**: Vision (testable hypothesis, not yet empirically confirmed in this project)

**Sources**:
- SCP Foundation fictional framework
- Memetics theory (Richard Dawkins)
- Information theory (Shannon, semiotics)
- Cognitive science (memory, attention, forgetting)

**Related questions**:
- Do antimemetic properties emerge from neural constraints or information structure?
- Is resistance-to-communication distinct from resistance-to-memory?
- Can antimemetic effects be measured?

### Information-Resistance (Vision)

**Working definition**: Any property of an entity/idea/signal that reduces the likelihood of accurate transmission, retention, or reproduction in a recipient.

Examples (Built):
- **Biotwang**: Mariana Trench whale call (verified Bryde's whale, 3.5s, 30Hz→8kHz metallic)
  - Difficult for humans to reproduce, remember, or vocalize
  - Natural phenomenon, not antimemetic (no intent)

Examples (Vision):
- **Gnostic ineffability**: Ultimate reality "beyond naming" (apophatic theology)
  - Theological resistance to language
  - Metaphorical vs literal: unknown

## 4. Verified Phenomena (Built)

### Biotwang (Verified)
- **What**: Mariana Trench underwater sound, ~3 seconds, 2014 discovery
- **Current identification**: Bryde's whale (Balaenoptera brydei/edeni) call
- **Frequency**: 30 Hz → 8 kHz metallic finale
- **Seasonality**: Feb–Apr and Aug–Nov migration peaks
- **Source**: IFLScience 2026-06-19 re-report of NOAA/Google AI 2024 finding
- **Status**: Built (verified; no anomalous source evidence as of 2026-09-07)
- **Use in mythology**: Liminal sound, frequency-to-color mapping (red → blue-white)

### Hydrothermal Vent Power (Built)
- **Endurance Energy** (Seattle): 100 kW hydrothermal generator off Oregon, deployed 2026-09-07
  - Fluids 750°F / ~400°C
  - Borehole ~200 ft from vent fields
  - Data via OOI Regional Cabled Array
  - Target: Tonga at MW scale
- **China UDC**: 24 MW wind-powered underwater data center (Shanghai, 10m depth, Oct 2025)
- **New**: Doldrums vent fields discovery (Mid-Atlantic Ridge, 2026-09-07, Schmidt Ocean Institute)
  - Two new hybrid hydrothermal fields
  - Fluids 280°C, rich anemone/crab/blind shrimp ecosystem
- **Status**: Built (verified current projects); vent power expanding rapidly

### Underwater Data Centers (Built)
- China's HiCloud project operating since ~Oct 2025
- Kraaken floating concept (Optimal Transit): 40 MW power, 60 MW AI compute, no land grid
- Status: China verified operational; Kraaken proposed/concept phase
